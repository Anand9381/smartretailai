# Frequently Asked Questions

## Ordering & Payment
- **Q: What payment methods do you accept?** A: UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net banking, Wallets, and COD
- **Q: Is my payment secure?** A: Yes, we use 256-bit SSL encryption for all transactions
- **Q: Can I change my order after placing it?** A: Yes, within 2 hours before processing
- **Q: Do you offer installment plans?** A: Yes, 3/6/12 EMI available for credit cards above ₹5000

## Account & Profile
- **Q: How do I reset my password?** A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page, check email
- **Q: Can I have multiple accounts?** A: Use different email addresses
- **Q: How do I update my profile?** A: Go to Account Settings after login
- **Q: Is my personal data safe?** A: We never share data with 3rd parties

## Discounts & Offers
- **Q: How do I get discounts?** A: Subscribe to newsletter, use coupon codes, track seasonal sales
- **Q: Are student/senior discounts available?** A: Contact support with valid ID
- **Q: Can I use multiple coupons?** A: Only one coupon per order
- **Q: When do sales happen?** A: Check "Deals" section, subscribe for alerts

## Tracking & Delivery
- **Q: How do I track my order?** A: Visit Orders page or click tracking link in email
- **Q: What if my package is lost?** A: File claim within 7 days, we'll reship or refund
- **Q: Can I change delivery address?** A: Only before item ships (within 2 hours)
- **Q: What's the return address?** A: Provided on prepaid return label

## Returns & Refunds
- **Q: What's your return window?** A: 7 days from delivery for most items
- **Q: How long for refund?** A: 5-7 business days after inspection
- **Q: Can I exchange instead of return?** A: Yes, free exchange for our errors
- **Q: What if the item doesn't fit?** A: Return within 7 days for refund

## Technical Issues
- **Q: Website not loading?** A: Clear cache, use Chrome/Firefox, check internet
- **Q: Can't login to my account?** A: Reset password, contact support if issue persists
- **Q: Is the app available?** A: Yes, download from Google Play / App Store
