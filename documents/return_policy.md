# Return & Exchange Policy

## Returns
- **7-day return window** from delivery date for most items
- Item must be unused, in original condition, with all packaging and accessories
- Personal care, electronics (opened), and gift cards are non-returnable
- Return shipping: Free for defective items, paid for buyer's remorse
- Refunds processed within 5-7 business days after inspection

## Exchanges
- Available for defective items, wrong item delivery, or size/color issues
- Free exchange shipping for our mistake, paid if buyer's request
- Stock availability determines if exchange is possible
- If item unavailable, full refund offered instead

## Refund Status
- Check refund status in your account under "Returns & Refunds"
- Refunds credited to original payment method
- Credit card/UPI: 3-5 business days
- Net banking: 5-7 business days

## Non-Returnable Items
- Personal care items (opened)
- Food & beverages
- Digital products
- Customized items
- Items purchased under clearance/final sale
