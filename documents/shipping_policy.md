# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Shipping Options
- **Standard Delivery**: 5-7 business days - Free on orders above ₹500
- **Express Delivery**: 2-3 business days - ₹99 extra
- **Next Day Delivery**: Available in metro cities - ₹199 extra
- Free shipping applies to order total, excluding delivery charges

## Delivery Timeframe
- Processing time: 1-2 business days
- Shipping begins after processing
- Holidays/weekends excluded from count
- Tracking updates via email and SMS

## Delivery Areas
- Pan-India coverage with exceptions
- Remote areas may take 10-14 days
- COD available in select areas
- International shipping: Contact support

## Issues & Resolution
- Delayed delivery: Contact support for status update
- Lost package: File claim within 7 days with proof
- Damaged packaging: Report on delivery, inspect before accepting
- Address correction: Update within 12 hours of order

## Cancellation During Shipping
- Can cancel before item ships (within 2 hours)
- Full refund if canceled before shipment
- Cannot cancel after shipped
