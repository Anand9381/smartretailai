# Warranty & Support Policy

## Warranty Coverage
- **Electronics (phones, headphones, watches)**: 6-12 months limited warranty
- **Accessories**: 3-6 months limited warranty
- **Home appliances**: 1-2 years limited warranty
- Covers manufacturing defects and hardware failure under normal use

## Warranty Does NOT Cover
- Physical damage, drops, cracks, or dents
- Water ingress or liquid damage
- Normal wear and tear
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Damage from wrong voltage/power supply
- Cosmetic damage

## Warranty Claim Process
1. Register claim in account within warranty period
2. Provide proof of purchase (order ID + invoice)
3. Ship item to service center (prepaid label provided)
4. Inspection: 3-5 business days
5. Repair/replacement: 7-14 business days
6. Free return shipping for warranty claims

## Extended Warranty
- Available for electronics at checkout
- Extends coverage by 6-12 months
- Covers accidental damage with small deductible
- Adds ₹299-₹999 depending on product
